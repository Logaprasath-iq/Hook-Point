The Heritage of HookPoint

Our Heritage, Mission & Standards

Born from a passion for the untamed Indian Ocean. We engineer, calibrate, and supply tournament-grade tackle that gives every angler the confidence to cast into punishing surf and reel in saltwater legends.

The HookPoint Story

Started on the Rocky Headlands of the Konkan Coastline.

In 2011, a group of dedicated coastal anglers grew frustrated by the fragile, mass-produced tackle imported into India. Saltwater would freeze bearings within three trips, guide rings cracked on beach pebbles, and generic rods buckled when monster Barramundi made their explosive first runs.

We started HookPoint in a small tackle repair shop on Pier 7. We began hand-wrapping Japanese Toray carbon blanks, substituting zinc hardware with marine-grade 316 stainless steel, and designing lures whose internal rattles trigger predatory strikes in turbulent monsoon waters.

“We don’t engineer tackle for trophy cabinets. We engineer tackle for the salt spray, the jagged barnacles, and the fight of a lifetime.”

India's 7,516 km coastline presents wildly contrasting hydrodynamics, rock structures, and game species. Here is how HookPoint gear masters each arena.

Our Tackle Solution:

12ft Apex 40T Toray parabolic blanks engineered for high kinetic tip recovery to throw 150g sinkers past crashing white water, paired with high-capacity surf spools.

Target: Threadfin Salmon, Bigeye Trevally, Guitarfish 120m+ Distance

Konkan & Malabar Mangrove Estuaries

Tidal backwaters flanked by dense mangrove root labyrinths, submerged granite snags, and fierce Barramundi and Mangrove Jack that strike hard and dive for cover.

Our Tackle Solution:

Fast-action 7ft Predator Pro rods with high-density backbone power, coupled with 60lb Japanese fluorocarbon leaders that withstand abrasive barnacle scraping.

Target: Barramundi, Mangrove Jack, Giant Trevally High Abrasion Resistance

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear, straightforward answers regarding our retail products, dispatch packaging, and warranty service.

Q Do HookPoint rods ship in protective rigid packaging?

Yes, absolutely. All 1-piece, 2-piece, and surf rods are shipped in heavy-gauge rigid PVC cylinders reinforced with high-density foam end-caps to guarantee zero transit damage during overland courier transport across India.

Q What is your 2-Year Saltwater Integrity Warranty?

If any HookPoint rod suffers blank failure under rated line tension, or if any reel gear or bearing seizes due to manufacturing seal defects within 24 months, we repair or replace it free of charge with door-to-door courier assistance.

Q Can I visit your Chennai outpost workshop in person?

Yes! Our flagship outpost is located on Marina Beach Road / ECR Coastal Highway, Chennai. You can visit us in person for hands-on gear demonstrations, custom rod flex tests, reel maintenance, or order collection.

Q Are your live and preserved baits safe for the marine environment?

Yes. Our StrikeScent preserved baits use food-grade sea salts, betaine, and natural amino acids. They are completely biodegradable, leave zero synthetic chemical residue in seawater, and are 100% fish-safe.

Q How do I submit an order enquiry without creating an account?

We eliminated mandatory logins. Simply add items or packages to your Tackle Box, click "Proceed to Order Enquiry" or visit our Order page, fill in your delivery details, and submit!

Q How should I maintain my HookPoint saltwater reel after a trip?

After every saltwater session, tighten your reel drag slightly to prevent moisture entering the drag stack, gently rinse with fresh lukewarm water under low pressure, shake off excess droplets, and loosen the drag completely before storing in a ventilated tackle bag.

Q Do you offer custom spooling and leader knot service?

Yes! For all reel orders and curated packages, our workshop team can pre-spool your reel under calibrated machine tension with your chosen AquaShield braided line and connect a fluorocarbon shock leader with a seamless FG knot ready to fish.

Q What payment methods and delivery timelines do you offer?

Upon submitting your order enquiry, our Chennai dispatch desk contacts you with a transparent pro-forma invoice supporting UPI, NEFT/IMPS, and card transfers. Metro orders arrive in 2–3 business days, while regional coastal locations take 3–5 business days.
