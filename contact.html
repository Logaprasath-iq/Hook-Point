Frequently Asked Questions

Q Can I visit your Chennai store to test rod action and reel balance?

Yes, our storefront in Chennai welcomes anglers during standard opening hours (Monday–Saturday 6 AM to 8:30 PM, Sunday 5:30 AM to 2 PM). You are welcome to test rod bend curves, evaluate reel weights, and inspect our terminal lures in person.

Q How fast are orders dispatched from your Chennai warehouse?

All in-stock products ordered before 3:00 PM IST are packaged in rigid protective tubes and dispatched on the same day. Deliveries within Tamil Nadu typically take 24–48 hours; other metro cities across India arrive in 2–4 business days.

Q How do I track an existing order enquiry?

You can track your order using the generated Order Reference number (e.g., HKP-2026-XXXX) by contacting our WhatsApp desk or choosing "Existing Order" in the enquiry form above.

Q What if an item arrives damaged in transit?

Every rod is packed in rigid high-impact PVC tubes. If transit damage occurs, notify us within 48 hours with a photo of the package, and we will dispatch a brand-new replacement immediately at zero additional expense.
