Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Q Can I customize or swap items inside a package?

Yes, absolutely. If you want a heavier braid rating, a left-hand retrieve reel, or specific hook sizes, simply specify your preference in the Special Instructions field on the Order Enquiry page, and our Chennai workshop team will configure it at no additional labor charge.

Q How does free shipping across India work?

All 4 curated packages automatically qualify for Free Express Pan-India Courier delivery. Fishing rods are packed inside reinforced rigid PVC cylinders with foam end-caps, while reels and terminal tackle travel in cushioned cartons.

Q What warranty comes with HookPoint packages?

Starter packages include 1-Year Full Coverage; Hunter and Surfcasting packages include 2 to 3 years of coverage against blank breakage and corrosion; our Ultimate Expedition package carries a Lifetime Hardware Guarantee.

Q How do I submit an order enquiry without creating an account?

We eliminated mandatory account logins. Simply click "Add Package to Order" on your preferred package, review your Tackle Box drawer, proceed to the Order page, enter your delivery address, and submit.
